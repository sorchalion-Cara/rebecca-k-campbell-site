About Rebecca

Writer of ancient worlds & present-day verse

I was born in the United Kingdom in 1956, though South Africa has claimed the better part of my life since: more than fifty years now, first amid the jacaranda-lined avenues of Pretoria, and since early 2022 in the sea-bright light of Cape Town, a city I have come to love without reservation.

History was one of my two majors at university, and my master's degree pursued Strategic Studies further still, a foundation that never quite left me. Ancient history, and the conduct of ancient war in particular, held and holds a fascination I was once fortunate enough to lecture on. Yet for the greater span of my working life I turned instead to fact rather than the distant past, as a science and technology journalist with an especial, though never exclusive, attention to aerospace.

Cape Town drew me for its nearness to both mountain and sea, and it is here that hiking became something close to a devotion. The photograph alongside was taken by a passing trail runner on the Steenberg Plateau, within Table Mountain National Park. Fencing followed not long after, answering a wish held since childhood; it remains, I think, the rare sport in which losing can feel almost as satisfying as victory.

After decades in service of fact, I turned a few years ago to the freer discipline of verse, composing short poems that now live on the Other Writings page. The Lay of the Vixens, my first sustained work of fiction, asked several years and five drafts of me before it would consent to be finished. A second book is already taking shape, this time within the stranger, more speculative terrain of science fiction.

Debut Novel

The Lay of the Vixens

A Song of Ancient Amazons

Amazons: for centuries the word summoned little more than travellers' rumour and the myth of a distant border: women who rode, hunted, and fought as equals in nations that could imagine such parity only as legend. The Lay of the Vixens gathers those fragments into song, following its riders from open steppe to the clamour of battle, and onward into the long, uncertain corridor between memory and myth. Available now, in paperback and as an e-book, wherever books are sold.
