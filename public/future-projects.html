A New Science-Fiction Novel

A second book is underway — a departure into science fiction, a genre whose speculative distances feel, to its author, oddly continuous with the ancient world she has just left behind. Particulars will surface here as the manuscript takes clearer shape; for now, only the fact of its becoming can be shared.

And, quietly, in the margins, a further thought is taking its own unhurried shape: whether some of the poems gathered under Other Writings might, in time, be drawn together into a small collection of their own. Nothing is decided, and nothing promised — only a possibility she is, for now, content to entertain.
