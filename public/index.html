About Rebecca

Writer of ancient worlds & present-day verse

I was born in the United Kingdom in 1956, but have lived for more than fifty years in South Africa, a country I have come to love. Most of that time I spent in the lovely city of Pretoria, but in early 2022 I moved to the even lovelier city of Cape Town.

History was one of my BA majors at university, and my MA was in Strategic Studies — I never lost my love of history, especially ancient history and ancient warfare, on which I once lectured. But I have spent the greater part of my working life as a science and technology journalist, covering especially (though far from exclusively) aerospace.

One reason I moved to Cape Town was to be close to both mountains and the sea. Since arriving, I've become a hiker — the photo alongside was taken by a friendly trail runner on the Steenberg Plateau, part of Table Mountain National Park — and fulfilled a lifelong dream of taking up fencing, which I love. Fencing must be the only sport where losing is almost as much fun as winning.

After decades of writing factual stories, I started writing short poems in free verse a few years ago; a few appear on the Other Writings page. The Lay of the Vixens, my first book, took some years and five drafts to write. I'm now in the early stages of a second work, which will be science fiction.
